Why is mine a little different?

The star does not only slide. It also wobbles a little, because we watch it from the Earth, and the Earth goes around the Sun. Five photos catch the slide and a bit of the wobble together. Measuring that wobble is the next step, and it tells us how far away the star is.

How far away is it?

Hold a finger up and close one eye, then the other: the finger jumps against the wall. The Earth does the same thing for us as it goes around the Sun, so a nearby star seems to wobble. is the size of that wobble. The smaller the wobble, the farther the star.

Parallax: how much a nearby star seems to shift as the Earth moves around the Sun. Astronomers measure it in milliarcseconds (thousandths of an arcsecond).
