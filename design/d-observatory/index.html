Why is mine a little different?

The star does not only slide. It also wobbles a little, once a year, because we watch it from the Earth and the Earth goes around the Sun. Five photos catch the slide plus a bit of that wobble. The wobble is the next step, and it tells you how far away the star is.

Fixed stars cancel out. The mover is left as a pair: bright where it is now, dark where it was.
