## Q3. Judging
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/ (VERIFIED). Each criterion scored 1 to 5 at every level.
- Impact: "addressing either a minor or major problem ... The extent to which it inspires or helps others varies, ranging from a broad audience to a select few." 5 = "addressing a significant problem ... reach is extensive ... could change the world."
- Creativity: "originality and innovation ... a novel concept that has not been attempted before or presents an improvement upon existing ideas in a meaningful way." 5 = "groundbreaking approach that has never been attempted before."
- Validity: "grounded in scientific principles and demonstrates technical, functional, or logical feasibility ... usability and user-friendliness of the project influence its practicality." 5 = "scientifically sound and technically feasible ... fully developed and ready for real-world implementation ... highly usable and user-friendly."
- Relevance: "fully and directly addresses the challenge ... meaningfully integrates NASA data, utilizing it as a core component ... rather than a superficial addition."
- Presentation: "clearly conveying the challenge, their proposed solution, and its significance. The storytelling was engaging ... Judges evaluate the demo and full project to compute this score." Guide urges equal attention to this criterion.
- Stages: Phase 1 Local/Universal judging picks Global Nominees (announced Dec 3, 2026). Phase 2 expert Global Judges pick Global Finalists + Honorable Mentions (Dec 17, 2026). Phase 3 executive committee (NASA + Space Agency Partners) picks winners (announced Jan 26, 2027). Universal Event projects are compared only against each other. Local awards are separate. Nov 16 judging begins (secondary source, UNVERIFIED: https://astrobiology.com/2026/09/04/registration-is-now-open-for-the-2026-nasa-space-apps-challenge/).
- Ten global awards (verbatim definitions): Best Use of Science "best and most valid use of the scientific method"; Best Use of Data "best makes space data accessible or leverages it to a unique application"; Best Use of Technology "most innovative use of technology"; Galactic Impact "most potential to improve life on Earth or the universe"; Best Mission Concept "most plausible concept and design"; Most Inspirational "captures our hearts"; Best Storytelling Award "most creatively communicates the potential of open data through the art of storytelling"; Global Connection Award "best connects people around the world through technology"; Art & Technology Award "most effectively combines technical and creative skills"; Local Impact Award "greatest potential for making a difference in their local community".
- Plausible fits for this challenge: Best Use of Data, Best Use of Technology, Best Use of Science, Art & Technology (blink video aesthetic), Best Storytelling. (opinion, flagged)

## Implications for the viewer
- Field is crowded (~144 repos, many with the same blink/flipbook concept and several live pre-hackathon). Mere blink-by-RA/Dec will not differentiate; Creativity is scored against existing ideas.
- Validity and Relevance reward real SPHEREx data via IRSA, correct handling (flags, per-pixel wavelength), and the five test targets working (esp. WISE 0855). Make all five one-click presets.
- Hard compliance items: public demo link (video <=30s OR <=7 slides, exceed = ineligible), public final-project URL with no login, English, at least one NASA data source listed, every library/asset in References, SI-use disclosure (not seen by judges), OSI license in repo (Apache 2.0 default), no under-18 likeness, submit before 11:59 p.m. local time Nov 15 and resubmit freely.
- Spend real effort on Presentation (equal weight): a 30s video showing WISE 0855 moving beats a feature list; demo-first landing page with URL coordinates (a stated consideration).
- Aim at Best Use of Data / Technology framing: accessibility for non-experts, easy to update with new releases, coordinate-in-URL embedding.
